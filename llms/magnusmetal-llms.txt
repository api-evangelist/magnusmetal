# MagnusMetal

> Magnus Metal (MagnusMetal Ltd.) is an Israeli advanced-manufacturing company that builds a
> patent-pending, volume-alloy Digital Casting System. A slicer designs a ceramic mold layer by
> layer and solid alloy is deposited into each layer, so parts are cast from widely available
> solid metals rather than powders and with no hard tooling. Founded 2017, headquartered at the
> Revadim Industrial Park, Kibbutz Revadim, Israel.

## Important for agents

returns a genuine 404, and the `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`, `portal.` and `app.` subdomains do
